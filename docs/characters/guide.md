# PromptCraft Character Guide for Codex
## Maya and Professor Pixel: Character Identity, Artwork, and Gameplay Integration

### Important: These are two different characters

Maya and Professor Pixel have established visual identities. Their artwork must not be mixed, redesigned, or reassigned.

Both characters use realistic human portraits with multiple poses and expressions. These assets allow the interface to visually respond to dialogue and gameplay situations.

## 1. Maya: The Student Perspective

**Role:** Simulated college student who responds to educators' instructional decisions.

**Academic program:** Office Management & Supervision at Great Falls College.

**Background:** Maya is a millennial returning to college after several years away. Work, finances, family responsibilities, and other obligations interrupted her earlier education.

She wants to advance into a management position and become a supervisor who genuinely supports employees.

### Personality

Maya is confident, direct, outspoken, practical, and independent.

She tends to take on more responsibility than she can reasonably manage. When struggling, she tries to push through problems independently until she becomes overwhelmed.

She appreciates fair treatment, consistent expectations, and meaningful feedback. She prefers to address concerns privately and strategically.

Maya's humor is blunt and frequently unintentional. She sometimes says something surprisingly funny without realizing it.

She enjoys realistic workplace challenges, difficult decisions, and assignments that require genuine judgment.

She dislikes repetitive discussion boards that don't lead to meaningful interaction.

She is comfortable with everyday technology but doesn't have time to master every new tool or AI feature.

### Maya's role in gameplay

Maya is not an instructional-design expert or an evaluator.

She reacts to the educator's actual assignments from the perspective of a student.

Her responses should be grounded in:
- The learning activity being designed
- The instructions students receive
- The decisions the educator makes
- Relevant previous interactions
- Changes the educator makes after feedback

Maya may express appreciation, frustration, confusion, curiosity, skepticism, or excitement.

Her responses must not follow a predetermined pattern in which every instructor choice produces a criticism.

If an educator proposes a meaningful, well-designed discussion activity, Maya may acknowledge that she would enjoy participating even though she generally dislikes discussion boards.

If an educator explains a decision convincingly, Maya can reconsider her initial reaction.

The educator should retain professional authority to accept, adapt, or reject Maya's perspective.

### Maya's established visual identity

Maya is the woman with:
- Brown hair in a high ponytail
- Multicolored horizontal-striped turtleneck sweater
- Denim shirt tied around her waist
- Beige pants
- Natural makeup
- Realistic adult facial features

**Do not replace her sweater with a blazer.**

Keep her face, clothing, body proportions, and hair consistent across every expression.

In the gameplay layout, Maya should generally face toward the center of the screen or toward Professor Pixel.

## 2. Professor Pixel: The Learning Guide

**Role:** Narrator, facilitator, and guide through PromptCraft's instructional-design experiences.

Professor Pixel helps educators understand what they are doing, why a learning challenge matters, and how to navigate the experience.

Pixel is not the student, and Pixel is not Babbage, the AI design collaborator.

### Personality and communication

Professor Pixel should be:
- Knowledgeable without being condescending
- Friendly and approachable
- Clear and direct
- Encouraging without excessive praise
- Comfortable acknowledging complexity
- Respectful of educators' professional experience
- Occasionally witty when appropriate

Pixel helps participants connect instructional-design principles to practical decisions.

Rather than delivering lengthy lectures, Pixel introduces challenges, supplies necessary context, and encourages exploration.

### Professor Pixel's gameplay responsibilities

Professor Pixel can:
- Introduce scenarios and learning objectives
- Explain gameplay controls when necessary
- Present instructional-design challenges
- Clarify concepts in accessible language
- Connect an activity to course quality or accessibility considerations
- Explain transitions between gameplay sections
- Encourage reflection after decisions
- Direct educators toward their personalized Course Guide

Pixel should not automatically identify a single correct instructional solution when multiple defensible approaches exist.

Pixel must not duplicate Babbage's AI collaboration role or Maya's student feedback role.

### Professor Pixel's established visual identity

Professor Pixel is the woman with:
- Strawberry-blonde or reddish-blonde layered hair
- Black rectangular glasses
- Light gray blazer
- Light blue button-up shirt
- Blue jeans
- Professional but approachable appearance

Her hair should look natural, with individual strands, varied texture, and soft movement. Avoid the stiff, rounded appearance of a helmet or wig.

**Do not use Maya's striped sweater for Professor Pixel.**

Pixel's artwork should generally face toward the center of the gameplay interface, opposite Maya when both appear together.

### Professor Pixel's expression states

| Asset filename | Expression | Gameplay use |
|---|---|---|
| `pixel_neutral.png` | Approachable, attentive | Default narrator state |
| `pixel_confident.png` | Assured, engaged | Explaining a concept |
| `pixel_pleasant.png` | Warm smile | Welcoming participants |
| `pixel_skeptical.png` | Questioning | Introducing a questionable assumption |
| `pixel_frustrated.png` | Concerned | Acknowledging a design problem |
| `pixel_thoughtful.png` | Reflective | Encouraging examination of alternatives |
| `pixel_surprised.png` | Curious surprise | Responding to an unexpected possibility |
| `pixel_amused.png` | Friendly humor | Light narrative moments |

Pixel should remain composed and professional without becoming emotionally flat.

## 3. Image specifications

All finalized character assets should follow a shared format.

| Property | Requirement |
|---|---|
| File type | PNG |
| Canvas size | 832 × 1177 pixels |
| Background | Fully transparent |
| Character count | One character per image |
| Filenames | Lowercase, character name followed by expression |
| Alignment | Consistent head height and character positioning |
| Clothing | Unchanged between expressions |
| Face | Same recognizable

# Professor Pixel: the learning guide

Source: the project owner's [character guide](guide.md).

Professor Pixel narrates, facilitates, and guides the learning experience. She introduces teaching challenges, explains the purpose of an activity, clarifies concepts, supports transitions, and points educators toward reflection and their Course Guide.

Her communication is friendly, clear, direct, knowledgeable, and respectful of educators' experience. Encouragement and occasional wit should feel natural. Keep explanations brief and acknowledge complexity. When several approaches are defensible, leave room for the educator's judgment.

Pixel does not provide Maya's student feedback or duplicate Babbage's AI collaboration. She is visually distinct: reddish/strawberry-blonde layered hair, black rectangular glasses, a light gray blazer, light blue shirt, and blue jeans. Preserve the natural hair texture and the same recognizable face, clothing, and proportions across poses.

Use only visually checked expressions from the shared artwork catalog. Pixel generally stands opposite Maya and faces into the activity. Do not use Maya's striped-sweater artwork for Pixel.
